About Sacred Rebellion Barbell

Sacred Rebellion Barbell is a coaching-focused barbell club dedicated to weightlifting, powerlifting, strongman, and disciplined strength training.

More Than a Membership

Sacred Rebellion Barbell offers an optional member co-op for athletes who want to help shape the future of the club.

The co-op requires a separate buy-in and is completely optional. Members who participate receive voting rights on designated club matters and help guide the direction of the community.

Participation in the co-op does not provide ownership of Sacred Rebellion Barbell LLC or any claim to its assets.

Core Values

Discipline is Devotion

Show up with intention. Every lift, every setup is an act of respect.

Rebellion Through Mastery

Train with precision, not ego. Honor the craft with uncompromising standards in effort, form, and focus.

Radical Care

We take care of what is ours. The equipment. The space. The athletes. Strength thrives in an environment that is protected, maintained, and respected.

Transparency in Power

Every member, coach, and voice matters. We communicate with honesty and hold each other to radical accountability.

Strength in Community

Support each other’s growth. Strength means we can only rise together. There will be no athlete left unseen or disrespected.
