Member Stories

Strength changes more than the number on the bar.

Syd

When I first joined a gym, I was nervous. Unsure of myself. I couldn’t do a pull-up or a sit-up. Then I met Sarah Stewart. She didn’t hype me up or hand out easy wins. She met me where I was, looked me in the eye, and told me to start. One rep. Then another.

Once, during a dead hang, I had my eyes shut tight. Sarah called out, “Syd, you can’t be intimidating with your eyes closed.” That statement took on many layers for me from that moment.

Since starting, I’ve lost forty pounds, regulated my diabetes, and found a steadiness I hadn’t felt in years. I can deadlift 230 pounds now. When life gets hard, I remember: I’ve already lifted harder things.

Megan

Sarah is an amazing coach. When I started my weightlifting journey 2 years ago with her I had no idea how to lift and have always been intimidated in the gym. She took the time to show me proper technique and form along with building my confidence in the gym.

After 2 years of lifting with her I have so much more muscle definition and strength which not only has improved my physical health but also my mental health. Sarah has truly changed my life!

Megan, 41-year-old working mom of 2

Sarah B.

I started training with Sarah a few years ago at a time when I was totally stuck. Sarah didn't just guide me in workouts, she gave me the holistic approach and tools to lose fat and build muscle in a safe and scalable way.

I’m the type of person who never did sports growing up, and calling myself an “athlete” made me cringe. But with Sarah, I’ve built the confidence I needed and seen results: nearly 30 pounds lost, major strength gains, and finally hitting PRs that felt impossible before.

Courtney

About Sacred Rebellion Barbell

Sacred Rebellion Barbell is a member-governed barbell club dedicated to weightlifting, powerlifting, strongman, and disciplined strength training.

More Than a Membership

Members who choose to join the co-op have a voice in shaping the future of the club. Sacred Rebellion Barbell isn't just a place you train. It's a place built by the hands that lift inside it.

Core Values

Discipline is Devotion

Show up with intention. Every lift, every setup is an act of respect.

Rebellion Through Mastery

Train with precision, not ego. Honor the craft with uncompromising standards in effort, form, and focus.

Radical Care

We take care of what is ours. The equipment. The space. The athletes. Strength thrives in an environment that is protected, maintained, and respected.

Transparency in Power

Every member, coach, and voice matters. We communicate with honesty and hold each other to radical accountability.

Strength in Community

Support each other's growth. Strength means we can only rise together. No athlete is left unseen or disrespected.
