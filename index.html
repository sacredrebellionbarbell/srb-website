About Sacred Rebellion Barbell

Sacred Rebellion Barbell is a member-governed barbell club dedicated to weightlifting, powerlifting, strongman, and disciplined strength training.

Core Values

  • Discipline is Devotion Show up with intention. Every lift, every setup is an act of respect.
  • Rebellion Through Mastery Train with precision, not ego. Honor the craft with uncompromising standards in effort, form, and focus.
  • Radical Care Respect the tools and the people who share them. Keep the space clean, maintained, and grounded in awareness.
  • Transparency in Power Every member, coach, and voice matters. We communicate with honesty and hold each other to radical accountability.
  • Strength in Community Support each other’s growth. Strength thrives when we rise together. No athlete left unseen or disrespected.
