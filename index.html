About Sacred Rebellion Barbell

Sacred Rebellion Barbell is a member-governed barbell club dedicated to weightlifting, powerlifting, strongman, and disciplined strength training.

More Than a Membership

Members who choose to join the co-op have a voice in shaping the future of the club. Sacred Rebellion Barbell isn't just a place you train. It's a place built by the hands that lift inside it.

Core Values

Discipline is Devotion

Show up with intention. Every lift, every setup is an act of respect.

Rebellion Through Mastery

Train with precision, not ego. Honor the craft with uncompromising standards in effort, form, and focus.

Radical Care

We take care of what is ours. The equipment. The space. The athletes. Strength thrives in an environment that is protected, maintained, and respected.

Transparency in Power

Every member, coach, and voice matters. We communicate with honesty and hold each other to radical accountability.

Strength in Community

Support each other's growth. Strength thrives when we rise together. No athlete left unseen or disrespected.
